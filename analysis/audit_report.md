## Data-quality notes

- 6,530 rows are flagged `no_stats_flag` (player appeared, recorded no offensive stat). Kept, contribute 0 to totals, excluded from per-player-game averages.
- `total_yards` and `total_tds` are player-level measures. Summing them across players double counts completions / passing TDs, so team and league totals use passing + rushing yards and rush + receiving + ST TDs.
- Postseason week numbers differ before/after 2021; findings use the regular season.
- Regular-season team-games per season: 2015: 512, 2016: 512, 2017: 512, 2018: 512, 2019: 512, 2020: 512, 2021: 544, 2022: 542, 2023: 544, 2024: 544, 2025: 544 (2022 is 542 because the BUF-CIN game was cancelled).
